PF2e Toolbox

Three tools for Pathfinder 2e (Remaster). Everything runs in your browser — nothing you type here leaves your device.

New to Pathfinder

What this game does differently, with live demos: degrees of success, the three-action turn, dying and recovery, the social ladder. Start here if you've played other tabletop games but not this one.

✦ Spellbook

All ten casting classes. Prepare the day's spells or build a repertoire, track castings and focus points, and see what a spell actually does at the rank you're casting it.

◆ Party Tracker

The party's passive DCs on one screen, secret checks rolled for everyone at once, exploration activities and marching order, and live HP and conditions. Imports straight from Pathbuilder.
